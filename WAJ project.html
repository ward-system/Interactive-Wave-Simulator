About Visible Light

Generally Safe
Is It Dangerous?

No, normal visible light is safe for humans. Only extremely bright concentrated beams (such as high-powered lasers or staring directly at the sun) can harm your eyes.

Where Is It Used?

Used for human vision, home and street lighting, photography, phone and TV screens, fiber-optic internet cables, and solar panels.
